How a swing gets scored

First it has to be a sword: a swing and miss on strike three, with bat tracking and a bat speed under 60 mph. No K, no sword. The score starts at 50 and adds:

  • Up to 50 for a dead bat. The slower the swing, the more points.
  • Up to 25 for swing tilt. A steeper path through the zone scores higher.
  • Up to 25 for swing length. A longer swing scores higher.

90 makes the board. 100 is elite.

What happens every morning

  1. A scheduled job reads the last few days of pitches from Baseball Savant.
  2. It keeps the swords, scores them, and finds each one's clip.
  3. It saves everything as plain data files, and the site redeploys itself.
  4. Your browser loads those files directly.

Why it's built this way

The first version ran across five services. In 2026 the API disappeared, the daily jobs got switched off for inactivity, and the database paused. A health check failed for days along the way, and nobody was reading it. The site just sat there saying it couldn't load.

So this version has no database and no server. A slow off-season has nothing to shut off, and if the daily job ever stops, it shows on this page and we hear about it.
